Dwallet

Delete your Dwallet account

Deleting your account permanently removes your Dwallet account, cloud backups, and account-linked activity. Items saved locally on a device are managed on that device.

Verify your account

Choose the Google account linked to Dwallet, or sign in with your Dwallet email and password.

Confirm account deletion

After deletion, open Dwallet on each device and connect to the internet. The app will ask whether to keep a separate local vault or delete that device’s copy.
